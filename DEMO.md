# WasteRecast demo and methodology

## Copy-paste test case: India source-grounded screening

Open https://wasterecast.web.app, select **India**, set city **Pune**, state **Maharashtra**, quantity **12** tonnes per month, and paste this in **What material do you have?**:

```text
Illustrative scenario, not a real factory manifest. A pharmaceutical reactor-cleaning line generates approximately 12 tonnes per month of spent wash solvent. The operator reports toluene and isopropyl alcohol in the stream, but has not supplied a laboratory assay, halogen analysis, water content, measured flash point, SDS, or current disposal and transport quotes. We want to investigate whether solvent recovery can turn this waste into a reusable industrial input. No recycler has confirmed acceptance or price.
```

Press **Profile with Gemini**. Review the material, quantity and missing facts, then press **Find recovery leads**. The first five cards show a listed process and location. Open **Evidence & checks** for the source and acceptance questions. Press **Ask Gemini to assess** for brief route hypotheses or **Ask Gemini about fit** on a card for an explanation. Select **Compare costs** to open that facility's calculator. The value remains **Pending quotes** until actual disposal cost and buyer and transport quotes are supplied. Presentation-only amounts must be labeled hypothetical. If App Check or the service is unavailable, the persistent connection message explains the failure; manual output is never presented as Gemini output.

## Copy-paste test case: worldwide discovery

Select **United Kingdom**, city **London**, leave state/province blank, quantity **5**, and paste:

```text
Illustrative scenario. Five tonnes per month of mixed post-industrial polypropylene offcuts are generated by a packaging plant. Resin grade, additives, contamination, color mix, and melt-flow index are unknown. We want to discover nearby industrial recycling leads and ask what evidence they need before claiming they can use this material.
```

Press **Profile with Gemini** and then **Find recovery leads**. The site queries live public OpenStreetMap industrial-recycling records and material-tagged recycling centres near London. Every map result remains **material fit unverified**. If a public Overpass server is busy, the site reports that fact rather than inventing results. This test depends on the external free API being available.

### Solid-material scenario: textile scraps

Select **United Kingdom**, enter **London** as the city, and paste: `Illustrative scenario: 4 tonnes per month of clean cotton garment cutting scraps from a clothing factory. Mixed colors; no coating known. Fiber blend and buyer acceptance are unverified.` Press **Screen manually**, then **Find recovery leads**. The app classifies the material as textile, shows repair/remanufacture and fiber recovery as possible routes inside the technical details, then searches live map leads. Generic industrial sites remain unverified; a biological-only site is rejected. If the map API is unavailable, no facility is fabricated.

## The AI methodology to explain to judges

1. **Multimodal evidence extraction.** Gemini 3.5 Flash reads the user description and optional manifest, SDS, or image. A JSON schema constrains it to material family, constituents, contaminants, hazards, flash point, explicitly stated tonnes per month, evidence quotes, confidence, and missing facts. Unknown properties stay null. It cannot create a lab assay or regulatory status.
2. **Source-backed candidate retrieval.** Firestore holds 912 facility records for plastics, metals, solvents, and oils extracted from three public Indian pollution board documents. Each keeps a source URL and PDF page. Other material families, including in India, use the selected city for a live geocoding and OpenStreetMap Overpass search for `industrial=recycling` sites and relevant material-tagged centres within 30 km. Map tags mean *discovery lead*; they do not prove material acceptance or commercial service.
3. **Hard screening before AI.** Reproducible code rejects a documented material conflict or a flash-point violation and retains every rejection reason. This prevents a language model from overriding a measured safety constraint. All other leads remain unverified until a facility supplies current authorization, specifications, capacity, and price.
4. **Gemini technical triage.** On request, Gemini reads the source snippets for up to eight screened leads and produces structured route hypotheses, source bases, and the key unanswered question for each. Open map records are always capped at weak evidence. A separate Gemini fit note explains an individual lead. Neither output certifies compliance or a transaction.
5. **Transparent economics.** The engine uses `12 × tonnes/month × (avoided disposal cost + buyer quote − transport quote − pretreatment quote)` in the selected country's currency. Missing commercial inputs leave the output pending. AI never supplies a price or carbon number.
6. **Human decision.** A downloadable dossier gathers the material description, source link, unanswered questions, and quote assumptions for a recycler and environmental professional to check before any shipment.

The innovation is the evidence chain: uncertain waste inputs become a structured technical profile, source-linked reuse leads, an AI-assisted question list, and a commercial case that refuses to invent missing facts. This is a working screening prototype. Actual waste movements require verified permits, assays, contracts, and local legal review.

## Free-tier architecture

```text
Firebase Hosting (Spark) → browser UI and animated recovery story
      ├─ Firebase AI Logic + Gemini Developer API (Spark) → profile and triage
      ├─ Cloud Firestore (Spark, read-only public catalog) → 912 India records
      ├─ Open-Meteo geocoding → city coordinate (free non-commercial API)
      ├─ OpenStreetMap Overpass → industrial sites and tagged recycling centres
      └─ PubChem PUG REST → chemical identity references
```

No Cloud Run, Cloud SQL, BigQuery, or paid Vertex AI service is used in this zero-spend deployment. Public Overpass and Open-Meteo availability is outside our control. For a production commercial service, these discovery dependencies need a licensed or self-hosted service and a verified facility onboarding pipeline.
