WasteRecast, built by Clorex for Sustainability & Social Impact, helps businesses find reuse counterparts and understand what must be verified before waste can become another business's raw material.

Verified Google/email accounts publish buyer requirements and seller offers in Cloud Firestore, with worldwide country filters, owner controls, consented business contacts and 30-day expiry. A separate discovery workspace supplies 912 source-attributed India board records and worldwide open-map leads. These records are not registered or verified buyers.

Gemini extracts material facts from descriptions and optional documents, explains semantic fit and powers Recast's conversational app actions. Hybrid retrieval combines material aliases, real 768-dimensional Gemini embeddings and curated process connections using reciprocal rank fusion. A reviewed evidence passport supports reproducible pass/fail/unknown checks across compatible units. Missing measurements remain unknown. Model scores cannot override hard conflicts. A small process solver identifies supported operations to investigate for a partial fit, requiring a trial, retest and written buyer acceptance. A separate landed-cost calculator uses referenced costs and usable yield without inventing prices.

Firebase Hosting serves the app. Firebase Authentication identifies owners. Firestore stores exchange listings, private profiles/reviews, the sourced catalog and durable embedding-budget counters. Firebase AI Logic connects extraction and explanations to the Gemini Developer API with App Check. A private Vercel service protects the Gemini key for verified-user embeddings and short-lived Gemini Live tokens. Cloud Run is not deployed; Firebase is the selected hosting path.

The updated three-minute demonstration shows the deployed public app, actual Gemini explanations, acceptance checks, a documented drying investigation, landed costs and the evaluation lab. Material properties, counterparts and prices are explicitly illustrative. The signed-out recording shows the labelled alias/process fallback; separate service tests verified actual embeddings. Narration uses the participant's own recorded voice.

Verification includes 57 automated tests, 24 authored acceptance cases, 29 cloud/access checks and 13 Firebase SDK checks. These are scoped software checks, not independently measured market accuracy. Expert review and verified sample/trade outcomes remain pending. Free quotas and third-party uptime limit availability. The initial retrieval pool is bounded to 200 records before pagination. No perfect match, certified compliance, realized savings or measured emissions reduction is claimed.
